About Me

Hey! My name’s Anthony Medrano, and I started Anthonys Anything Services with one goal: to earn as much as I could to help pay for out-of-state college tuition, rent, and all of life’s expenses. What began as a one-man operation is now a growing team of hardworking, dedicated high schoolers who share the same drive — to work hard, save for the future, and help our neighbors with whatever they need. I currently am a Sophomore at the University of Iowa studying Business Management. I am a proud graduate of Oconomowoc High school. My goal is to continue to recruit hustling teens that are handworkers aiming to save up for school, while also learning and performing lifelong skills.
